  <h1>About and philosophy</h1>
  <p class="lede">Working meetings on Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope data. The Roman Science Collaboration organizes them so people from many institutions can spend a few concentrated days on the data.</p>
</header>

<article class="prose">
  <h2>An unconference, on purpose</h2>
  <p>The people in the room build the agenda, in person or online. A Rally opens with proposals, not invited talks: a plot, a cross-match, a calibration that looks wrong, a question that needs a different statistic. Groups form, and the shared schedule is expected to move. Check-ins are a few minutes. The hours after that are for the work.</p>

  <h2>Projects</h2>
  <p>Bring a plan, or join one. Projects split, merge, or stop. A good Rally ends with a collaboration that did not exist at the start: a draft, a repository, coauthors, or a clearer question.</p>
  <p>Substantial work earns an invitation onto the papers. If you accept that help, offer the invitation. Share notes and code. Leave at home any dataset or library you cannot share. Some projects become Roman Science Collaboration papers. Some stay ordinary papers among the authors. Which path, and the RSC publication rules, are <strong class="tbd">TBD</strong>.</p>

  <h2>Who it is for</h2>
  <p>Small enough that a student can ask a basic question and be heard. When a room fills, organizers aim for a mix of skills, topics, and career stages, with space for early-career participants. You do not need to be the Roman person in your department. Work in the open, and credit the people next to you.</p>
  <p>RSC membership is voluntary and strongly encouraged. How to join, including student sponsorship, is on the RSC <a href="https://outerspace.stsci.edu/spaces/RSCPUB/pages/286851875/Roman+Science+Collaboration+RSC+Public+Page+Home">site</a>. Joining and applying are separate steps. Follow the <a href="{{ '/code-of-conduct/' | relative_url }}">code of conduct</a>.</p>

  <h2>Who makes a Rally happen</h2>
  <p>An Organizing Committee handles the purpose, the application, the balance of the participant list, the room, a skeleton schedule, building access, and how to report a problem in person. Organizers for the first Rally are <strong class="tbd">TBD</strong> and will be listed on the <a href="{{ '/events/rally2026/' | relative_url }}">2026 Rally</a> page.</p>

  <h2>Hosts</h2>
  <p>Any institution can host. The commitment and how to sign up are in <a href="{{ '/handbook/#what-it-means-to-host-a-rally-room' | relative_url }}">what it means to host a Rally Room</a>. Questions go through the <a href="{{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}">contact page</a>.</p>
