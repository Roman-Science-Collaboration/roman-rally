# Participation and FAQ

Dec 14-18, 2026, online and in person. Read the [about page]({{ '/about/' | relative_url }}) and the [code of conduct]({{ '/code-of-conduct/' | relative_url }}) before you apply. Applying means you will follow both.

## Questions

### Who is a Rally for?

Anyone working on Roman commissioning data, in person or on Zoom: observers, theorists, statisticians, software people, and students, including people with no prior Roman experience.

### Do I have to be a member of the RSC?

No. Membership is voluntary and strongly encouraged. Sponsorship for graduate students is on the [RSC public page](https://outerspace.stsci.edu/spaces/RSCPUB/pages/286851875/Roman+Science+Collaboration+RSC+Public+Page+Home). Joining and applying are separate steps.

### Do I need a finished project?

No. Bring a question, a worry, or a skill. Or join a plan on the first morning.

### Will there be talks?

A short orientation, brief check-ins, and maybe a few expert tutorials. The rest of the time is for working.

### How do I apply?

The [participant sign-up form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/1KZgV9BmCQiFieGkorWnvCNWMoyuz-FSELj9YmTKGAtw/viewform) opens Monday Nov 2, 2026. The deadline and the decision date will be posted on the [2026 Rally]({{ '/events/rally2026/' | relative_url }}) page when the form opens.

### How are people chosen?

If there are more applicants than seats, organizers mix skills, interests, and career stages, with attention to early-career astronomers. Written criteria will be posted on the event page before the form opens.

### Can I attend remotely?

Yes, on Zoom and Slack. In person is valuable, not required. More than three people at one institution should host a Rally Room.

### Is there funding?

There is no registration fee and no funding support.

### What should I bring?

A laptop, notes you are willing to share, and code you are free to show. Data and software you cannot share should stay at home.

### What data will we be allowed to use?

All public data products are in bounds. The mission sets the rules. The exact list will be posted on the [2026 Rally]({{ '/events/rally2026/' | relative_url }}) page once those products are set.

### Can my institution host a Rally Room?

Yes. Same time, approximately the same schedule, for people who cannot travel as far. You need a small meeting space and some local advertising. See [what it means to host a Rally Room]({{ '/handbook/#what-it-means-to-host-a-rally-room' | relative_url }}). Responses are due Nov 10 on the [host form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/1ovYDq2ZmVzcV_1i-PXOV6LtKhW3Cnx8XK-7muMQbAiA/viewform).
