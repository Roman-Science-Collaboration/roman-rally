The first Roman Rally is the opening meeting of the series. It will be a small, in-person working gathering on data from commissioning and the mission's first look at the sky. People will propose projects on the first morning, sort into groups, and spend the rest of the meeting writing code, making plots, and comparing notes.

The date is not chosen yet. It should land when there is something real to work on and a host room to work in. Roman launched on 30 August 2026, and NASA has said it expects to release the first images by early 2027. That season is useful context. The meeting date will be posted here when the organizers choose it.

Applications are not open. Please do not book travel from this page.

## What the days are for

Bring a question, a plot you want to try, or a skill you are willing to lend. You do not need a finished project, and you do not need to be the Roman expert in your department. The fuller account of how a Rally runs is on the [about page]({{ '/about/' | relative_url }}).
