The first Roman Rally focuses on commissioning data and the mission's first look at the sky. On the first morning people propose projects, form groups, and spend the rest of the week writing code, making plots, and comparing notes. The participant sign-up form is open.

## Dates, place, and host

- Dates: <strong>2026 Dec 14-18</strong>
- Venue: <strong>Rally Rooms (virtual and in-person) listed below</strong>

Responses to the [host form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/1ovYDq2ZmVzcV_1i-PXOV6LtKhW3Cnx8XK-7muMQbAiA/viewform) are ideally due by Oct 30 (late host submissions are accepted). The commitment is in [what it means to host a Rally Room]({{ '/handbook/#what-it-means-to-host-a-rally-room' | relative_url }}).

## How to apply

The <a href="https://docs.google.com/forms/d/1KZgV9BmCQiFieGkorWnvCNWMoyuz-FSELj9YmTKGAtw/viewform">participant sign-up form</a> is open. The deadline and the decision date will be posted here.

Which data products are in bounds, and under what rules, will be posted once the mission sets the public products for the week. The mission sets those rules. A Rally does not grant data rights.

Everyone at the meeting, including organizers, follows the [code of conduct]({{ '/code-of-conduct/' | relative_url }}).

## What the days are for

Bring a question, a plot you want to try, or a skill you are willing to lend. You do not need a finished project, or to be the Roman expert in your department. How a Rally runs is on the [about page]({{ '/about/' | relative_url }}).
