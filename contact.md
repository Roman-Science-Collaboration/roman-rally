  <h2>This website</h2>
  <p>To correct a page, use the "Edit this page" link in the footer, or open an issue or pull request in the <a href="https://github.com/Roman-Science-Collaboration/roman-rally">roman-rally repository</a>. The <a href="https://github.com/Roman-Science-Collaboration/roman-rally/blob/main/README.md">README</a> explains both paths. Small edits from people at any institution are welcome.</p>

  <h2>The collaboration and the mission</h2>
  <p>Roman Rally is a community site for meetings organized by the Roman Science Collaboration. The RSC's public home is on <a href="https://outerspace.stsci.edu/spaces/RSCPUB/pages/286851875/Roman+Science+Collaboration+RSC+Public+Page+Home">Outerspace at STScI</a>. Status of the Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope is published by NASA on the <a href="https://science.nasa.gov/blogs/roman/">Roman blog</a>.</p>
